Privacy & data protection

Privacy Policy

At New York Window Setup, we respect your privacy and are committed to handling your personal information responsibly and transparently. This policy explains how we collect, use and protect information when you visit our website or contact us.

Information we collect

We may collect information such as:

  • Your name, phone number, and email address when you contact us or request a quote.
  • Service preferences and project details you provide in our inquiry forms.
  • Basic website usage information such as browser type, device, and referral source.

How we use your information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Respond to your inquiries and provide service estimates.
  • Manage scheduling and service coordination.
  • Improve website content, usability, and customer communication.
  • Comply with legal requirements and protect against fraud or misuse.

Data sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We may share data only with trusted service providers that help us operate our business, such as communication tools or analytics services, and only when necessary to provide our services.

Cookies

Our website may use cookies or similar technologies to understand how visitors use the site and to improve the experience. You can disable cookies in your browser settings if you prefer.

Security

We use reasonable administrative and technical safeguards to help protect personal information from unauthorized access, alteration, or disclosure.

Your choices

You may request to update, correct, or delete personal information you have shared with us, or you may ask us not to contact you for marketing or follow-up purposes.
